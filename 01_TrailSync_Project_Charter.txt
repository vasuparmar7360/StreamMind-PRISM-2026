Project: TrailSync - Offline Field Coordination Platform

1. Executive Summary
TrailSync is a prototype platform for small field teams that need to coordinate tasks, observations and handover summaries. For the pilot, SQLite is the confirmed primary data store. The design should keep persistence behind a service boundary so that the storage engine can be revisited later.

2. Architecture Decisions
Backend service: Node.js with NestJS
Mobile client: Flutter
Pilot field site: Ramanagara test zone
Pilot review date: 22 November 2026
React Native was discussed but not chosen. Redis may be considered in the future but is not selected.
